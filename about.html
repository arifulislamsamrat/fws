About us

Fajr Web Services started as a hosting provider. It became something more once our customers kept asking us to build the things they hosted with us. Today we run cloud infrastructure, ship AI products, and embed our engineers in client teams — under one roof.

We do. We're the team you call when you want hosting that just works and the engineers who can rebuild it with you when "just works" isn't enough.

Our story

Seven years, four acts.

2019

One server, two engineers

We started reselling shared hosting and registering domains for friends. The first 50 customers came from word of mouth.

2021

Owned infrastructure

Migrated off resold hosting onto our own bare-metal fleet. Launched cPanel, LiteSpeed, and Cloudflare-integrated plans.

2023

Cloud & DevOps practice

Customers started asking us to deploy their apps, not just host them. We hired SREs. We learned Kubernetes the hard way.

2026

AI engineering, forward-deployed

Today we ship custom agents and embed engineers in our clients' teams. Same hosts. New depth.

Principles

Four things we won't compromise on.

①

Operate the boring layer flawlessly.

If your DNS, SSL, backups, and uptime aren't perfect, nothing else we do matters. That's the floor, not the ceiling.

②

Talk to humans.

No tier-1 chatbots. Every support conversation reaches an engineer who can read your logs.

③

Ship code, not slides.

Our deliverables are pull requests, dashboards, and running services — not 90-page strategy decks.

④

Roll off cleanly.

Every engagement has an exit plan. We document, we hand over, and your team owns what we built.
